IELTS Live Skills Mastery Program

For ambitious IELTS candidates seeking personalized, results-driven coaching, this program offers live, interactive training to elevate all four language skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking.

Sessions are conducted online in 60-minute lessons, typically booked in five-session blocks, ensuring focused progression, continuity, and measurable improvement. Each block is carefully structured to maximize efficiency and accelerate your path to your target band score.

Every session is tailored to your current level and objectives, ensuring you make consistent, measurable progress. With structured blocks and expert-led instruction, this program provides the ultimate combination of guidance, accountability, and strategy to maximize your IELTS performance.

IELTS Independent Study Program

After taking a placement test, if your current general English level is close to your target score, you can confidently enrol in this course.

This comprehensive self-paced Canvas Writing course is designed for independent learners who want structured, effective preparation without the need for a full-time tutor. You can study anytime, anywhere, and progress according to your own schedule.

The program goes beyond advanced grammar and essential vocabulary for Writing Task 1 or/and Task 2. You will receive step-by-step training in structure, task achievement, coherence and cohesion, vocabulary development, and grammatical accuracy.

Each lesson includes clear explanations, practical models, and focused exercises. After every instructional section, you will complete structured assessments designed to help you identify weaknesses and improve your ability to apply techniques accurately and confidently.

After completing the Writing Task 1 and Task 2 modules, you may evaluate your progress by taking an IELTS Writing Mock Test, assessed according to official IELTS criteria.

If at any point during the course you feel you need additional clarification, you also have the option to book a one-off support session for personalized explanation and Q&A on any topic covered in the program. Please note that these individual sessions are optional and available for an additional fee.

This course offers the ideal balance of flexibility, structure, and optional support — giving you full control over your preparation while ensuring you stay on track toward your target score.
