Hedging in Academic Writing

A ready-to-teach lesson that helps students notice, categorise and use hedging language, so their claims sound cautious, credible and academic. It starts with discovery tasks and moves through to a short writing task.

Preview

Lesson outline

  1. Warm-up: which statement sounds more academic?
  2. Noticing: spot the hedging language in four research sentences
  3. Concept: what hedging is and why academics use it
  4. Discovery: sort hedging devices into categories
  5. Input: modal verbs, tentative verbs, adverbs and phrases, with examples
  6. Practice: rewrite strong claims with appropriate hedging, then compare versions
  7. Accuracy: the problem of over-hedging
  8. Application: write and peer-review a short hedged paragraph
