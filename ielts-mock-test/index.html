IELTS Mock Test

Rehearse test day before test day.

The IELTS Mock Test is conducted under conditions that closely replicate the official IELTS exam. The test format, scoring system, timing, and level of difficulty are all aligned with the real IELTS exam. Many IELTS candidates choose to take a mock test to become more familiar with the exam environment and to better prepare for test day.

Speaking · $30

Online IELTS Speaking Mock Test

Format

  • 11–14 minute speaking interview
  • Immediate band score provided after the test
  • 10-minute consultation session with the examiner to review strengths and weaknesses and provide guidance for improving your speaking score
  • Video recording of the interview sent to the candidate

Listening to your own recorded performance is one of the most effective ways to improve pronunciation, fluency, and overall speaking performance.

Requirements & Conditions

  • A stable internet connection and access to Zoom or Google Meet are required.
  • After registration, you will be contacted within 24 working hours to schedule your interview time.
  • Your selected interview date must be within one week of registration.

To check availability for your preferred time, you may contact me via email (please include your full details, preferred date, and time) or complete this form and I’ll get back to you in 24 hours.

Writing · $25 / $35

Online IELTS Writing Mock Test

This test includes both Writing Task 1 and Task 2.

Feedback Options

  • Without detailed comments – $25
  • With detailed comments – $35. Includes an audio file from the examiner explaining strengths, weaknesses, and practical strategies to improve your Writing band score.

All writing scripts are assessed according to official IELTS marking criteria. This service is designed to familiarize candidates with the exam format, expectations, and scoring standards while helping them improve their performance effectively.

Important Notes & Conditions

  • After registration, you will be contacted within 24 working hours to confirm your test type (Academic or General Training).
  • Candidates must allocate 20 minutes for Task 1 and 40 minutes for Task 2.
  • The timing must strictly include the full writing process (start to finish), just like the real exam. Time spent transferring answers is included within this limit.
  • Responses must be handwritten. After completion, you should scan or take a clear photo of your writing and send it via WhatsApp.
  • Results will be delivered within 48–72 working hours after submission.

You may contact me via email (please include your full details, preferred date, and time) or complete this form and I’ll get back to you in 24 hours.
