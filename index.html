AI apps & resources

Small, smart tools that help students think, not just get answers.

AI-supported apps for academic English. Each one targets one real skill students need at university, gives feedback that makes them do the thinking, and runs in any browser with no sign-in. Student work stays on their own device.

ListeningLive

DAVE Notes: Lecture Note-Taking Trainer

Students watch a short TED talk or listen to a news story and take notes their way. Then they check their own notes against the key ideas, answer questions from their notes and set a goal.

Key ideasSignpostingSelf-checking

GrammarLive

Grammar Self-Correction Trainer

The app shows where each error is and what type it is, using correction codes teachers already use, like VF (verb form). Students do the fixing, and hints step up only when they ask for one.

Self-correctionError codesStep-by-step hints

About me

Teaching, assessment and academic leadership.

I am a CELTA and DELTA qualified English language educator with over 15 years of experience across higher education, ELICOS, and international assessment contexts. I currently teach at Macquarie University College and UTS College, supporting students who are preparing for university study and high stakes English examinations.

My qualifications underpin my approach to lesson design, language analysis, and principled teaching practice, and over the years I have developed a broad skill set that connects teaching, assessment, and academic leadership.
