Design approach

Three principles shape every app.

01

Feedback that makes students think

The apps give hints, codes and comparisons instead of correct answers, so students do the thinking and remember more.

02

One skill, done well

Each tool targets a specific academic skill, like signposting, paragraph structure or hedging, and fits into existing lessons or self-study.

03

Open and private

No accounts and no installs: open the link and start. Student work stays on their own device.

About

I design learning experiences that use AI to add real educational value. The focus is on helping students build lasting skills rather than handing them shortcuts.
