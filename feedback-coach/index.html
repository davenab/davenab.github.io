Free AI tutor for EAP and university students

From “improve cohesion” to I can do this myself.

Teacher feedback often names a problem without showing how to fix it. This coach explains what the comment means, helps you find the exact skill behind it, and teaches it step by step until you can use it on your own.

Open the coach ↗How it works
New · Free AI tutor

Got feedback you don’t understand? Turn it into a skill.

Paste a comment like “improve cohesion”. The coach explains it, finds the exact skill behind it and teaches it step by step. It never fixes your essay for you, so next time you can do it yourself.

What it covers

Three skills that come up in feedback all the time.

Each skill is broken into small parts, and every part has its own lesson, worked example, guided practice and independent task. The core lessons were written and checked by Dave. For other areas (grammar, academic style, critical analysis, referencing), the AI builds a lesson with the same steps and marks it clearly.

Cohesion and coherence

Linking words, reference words (it, this, they), substitution, ellipsis, word links, sentence flow and logical order.

ConjunctionReferenceLexical cohesionCoherence

Paraphrasing

Understanding the source, key information, synonyms, word forms, restructuring, avoiding patchwriting, keeping certainty and citing.

PatchwritingRestructuringHedgingCitation

Paragraph development

Topic sentences, explanation, elaboration, evidence, examples, interpreting evidence, linking to the argument and unity.

Topic sentenceEvidenceInterpretationUnity
Designed for learning

A tutor, not an essay writer.

It teaches, then checks

Hints come before answers. You only see “demonstrated independently” after you complete a new task without help, not just because you finished a lesson.

It won’t rewrite your work

Ask it to rewrite your essay and it will bring you back to the skill. You can practise on a short extract from your own writing and make the changes yourself.

Your data stays yours

Progress is saved only in your browser. Text you submit for feedback goes to Claude through your own account. Dave can’t see it. Remove names and student numbers first.

Getting started

How to open it

  1. Open the coachIt runs on claude.ai, so it opens in a new tab.
  2. Sign in to ClaudeUse any email: personal, Gmail or university. A free account is enough.
  3. Allow it to use ClaudeThe first time you ask for feedback, Claude asks for permission. The feedback uses your own free Claude allowance.
