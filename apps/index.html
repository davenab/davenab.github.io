Learning design × AI in education

Small, smart tools that help students think, not just get answers.

AI-supported apps for academic English. Each app targets one real skill students need at university, gives feedback that makes them do the thinking, and runs in any browser with no sign-in.

The apps

New apps are added as they’re built and tested with students.

ListeningLive

DAVE Notes: Lecture Note-Taking Trainer

Students watch a short TED talk or listen to a news story and take notes their way. Then they check their own notes against the key ideas, answer questions from their notes and set a goal.

Key ideasSignpostingSelf-checking

GrammarLive

Grammar Self-Correction Trainer

The app shows where each error is and what type it is, using correction codes teachers already use, like vf (verb form) or art (article). Students do the fixing, and hints step up only when they ask for one.

Design approach

Three principles shape every app.

Feedback that makes students think

The apps give hints, codes and comparisons instead of correct answers, so students do the thinking and remember more.

One skill, done well

Each tool targets a specific academic skill, like signposting, paragraph structure or hedging, and fits into existing lessons or self-study.

Open and private

No accounts and no installs: open the link and start. Student work stays on their own device.
