# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences, weighted equally. The home page's "Who are you?" chooser splits them:

- **Students**: international students preparing for university (EAP, direct-entry, first year) or IELTS. They use the free apps to practise one academic English skill at a time, and buy self-study packs.
- **Teachers**: EAP/ESL teachers who want zero-prep lesson kits, whole courses and free single slides, worksheets and Quizlet sets.
- **Coaching clients**: students who book live IELTS coaching, IELTS mock tests or General English courses (Speaking Fluency, Level Up) through `/book/`.

Most readers are English learners, often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

davenab.com is Dave Nab's teaching site: free AI-supported practice apps for academic English, ready-made resources for students and teachers, and IELTS / General English coaching.

Success means:
- more students practising with the apps and improving (hub pre/post scores, `api.davenab.com/stats`);
- resource sales to teachers and students (lesson kits, courses, self-study packs).

## Positioning

- **Makes students think.** Feedback makes the student do the work instead of handing over answers: test, teach, test; noticing and guided discovery; deliberately strict answer checking ("when torn, choose the lower level").
- **Free, private, no sign-in.** Apps run in any browser with no account. Student work stays on the device (localStorage at most). AI feedback is optional and only on request, and the app still works without it. Student text is never stored or logged.
- **Teacher and examiner expertise.** Built by a CELTA and DELTA qualified teacher and experienced IELTS speaking examiner with 15+ years in Australian higher education (Macquarie University College, UTS College).

## Operating Context

- Students use apps on their own, on laptops and phones, between classes or while preparing for a course or test. Teachers also run apps in class (e.g. Level Check result codes pasted into `#/class`).
- Teachers browse resources to plan lessons; paid items are bought through a PayPal link per item, which delivers the file automatically.
- Coaching clients compare courses and mock-test formats, then submit the booking form (sent to Dave's Google Form).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages from `main`; no build step at deploy. Main pages are generated by `_tools/build.py`; each app is a self-contained page in its own folder.
- The repo is public: no API keys, no paid files in the repo.
- AI goes through the Cloudflare Worker `api.davenab.com` with per-route daily caps on the free plan; always optional.
- Apps must work without sign-in, on laptops and phones, in light and dark mode.
- Analytics: cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics on pages and apps; opt-in anonymous hub scores are the one approved data exception.
- Draft content (hub units, Dictogloss passages, Level Check) stays marked unverified until Dave checks it.
- Planned apps: skim-and-scan timer, paragraph X-ray, synthesis builder, register swapper. Several resources are "Coming soon".

## Product Principles

1. Students do the thinking; tools give feedback, not answers.
2. Free and private by default: no sign-in, data on the device, AI only when asked for.
3. Every claim rests on real teaching and examining expertise or real evidence.
4. Each audience sees only what is made for it.
5. Works for a learner on a phone as well as on a laptop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Readers are English learners: plain language and clear error messages.
- Must work on phones and laptops, in light and dark mode, with no sign-in.
